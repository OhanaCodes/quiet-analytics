# For your own privacy page

Paste and adjust. Written plainly on purpose: a privacy notice nobody can read is not a notice.
Delete any line that is not true of your installation.

---

**When you visit this website**

We use our own server logs and nothing else. No Google Analytics, no third-party tracker, no
analytics cookies, and no script that reports your visit to anyone.

The log keeps a shortened network address — the last part of your address is removed before
anything is written down, so the record cannot be traced to one connection. It keeps the page,
the time, the response, and the site you arrived from, without that site's page or search terms.
Browser details are not kept.

Logs rotate daily and NN days are kept. A private summary is rebuilt nightly; it counts requests,
not people.

---

Two honest notes, for you rather than your visitors:

- Say **shortened** or **masked**, not "anonymous". A masked address is much weaker evidence than a
  full one, but it is still data about a visit. Claiming more than you can prove is the thing this
  tool exists to avoid.
- If your pages load fonts, maps or scripts from another company, those requests are separate from
  your server and your notice should say so.
